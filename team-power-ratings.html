How to read this

Every rating is a net rating: points per 100 possessions above an average team, built from shooting-luck-adjusted results. Teams are ranked by Current strength. Click a team for its offense/defense breakdown and who is out.

Current
Full strength minus projected-rotation players who are out right now (missed the team's last 3 games in a row, or marked out by hand). Best guess at how good the team is tonight.
Full
The team at full strength: its preseason projection updated by every game so far, adjusted for opponent strength, home court and the team's own missing players. The projection counts like 10 games, so early results move it gradually.
Season
Luck-adjusted results as played, no other adjustment.
Sched-adj
Season results with home court and each opponent's strength removed (the opponent as it actually lined up that night, missing players included).
Preseason
The starting point: the win projections' NERD + DARKO average with depth-chart minutes.
Change
Current minus Preseason.

A missing player's projected minutes are valued at replacement level instead of his rating, so missing a star costs far more than missing a bench player. Projected rotations come from the depth charts; players out long-term are best removed there.
