Methodology notes. Net Picks = 7 base own picks per round + picks received from other teams (transfers, multi-team arrangements, conditionals) − picks owed out (same types). Swap rights are excluded from both sides of the count since they exchange one pick for another. Swap Rights = incoming arrangements where the team holds the right to swap their pick for another team's pick and keep the better result. Top-5 Eligible = incoming 1st round picks from other teams whose protection clauses do not prevent the pick from conveying in the top 5 selections. A pick "protected 1–4" is eligible (can convey at #5); "protected 1–5" is not (conveys at #6 or later). Data source: RealGM Future Draft Pick Details, as of 2026-05-31. Traded pick obligations only; each team additionally holds their own undisclosed picks for all years/rounds not listed as outgoing.
