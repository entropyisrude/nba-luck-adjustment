Drag players between position columns or reorder within a column to set depth. Type any minutes value you want -- nothing auto-adjusts as you go. The Team Minutes total turns red once it's off 240 and green when it's back on the dot; click "Scale to 240" to proportionally rescale everyone's minutes back to 240 with the smallest change to each player's current number (position isn't a factor -- it's often wrong in the source data). Defaults for most teams are hand-tuned rotations; other teams are seeded from each player's 2025-26 MPG when we have it (shown in blue under their Min box), falling back to cap hit for rookies or data gaps. "Reset to Estimated" shows the raw MPG/cap-hit estimate. Your edits here are session-only — reloading returns to the default.
